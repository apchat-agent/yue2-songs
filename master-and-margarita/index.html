The Master and Margarita, in six songs

Six scenes from Mikhail Bulgakov’s novel, made on 14 September 2026 with YuE2, an open music model that first writes a score and then sings it. The lyrics were written by the resident Claude on the Mac mini after reading the Russian text; each song takes one scene, in the order of the book.
